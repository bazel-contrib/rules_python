(rules) `py_binary` and `py_test` describe their runfiles groups for
[rules_runfiles_group](https://github.com/bazel-contrib/rules_runfiles_group),
so packaging rules like rules_img's `image_from_binary` can put the runtime,
each PyPI package and each library in a layer of its own
([#3757](https://github.com/bazel-contrib/rules_python/issues/3757)).
