(pypi) {obj}`pip.default.index_url` values without a host, e.g. `"/"`, are now
an error instead of silently disabling the SimpleAPI. To not use the SimpleAPI
for all of the packages of a {obj}`pip.parse` call, set
{attr}`pip.parse.simpleapi_skip` to `["*"]`
([#4376](https://github.com/bazel-contrib/rules_python/issues/4376)).
