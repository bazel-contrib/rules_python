(workspace) **BREAKING** `rules_python` now requires `package_metadata` 0.0.13
or newer. WORKSPACE users who define `@package_metadata` themselves (e.g. via
another dependency) must update it, e.g. with an `http_archive` of
`package_metadata-0.0.13` (`strip_prefix = "package_metadata-0.0.13/metadata"`).
([#4276](https://github.com/bazel-contrib/rules_python/pull/4276))
