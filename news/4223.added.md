(pypi) The unified `@pypi` hub now generates a `requirements.bzl` with the
per-package macros of a concrete hub's (`requirement`, `whl_requirement`,
`data_requirement` and `dist_info_requirement`). A repo that renames its hub
away from the reserved `pypi` name keeps its
`load("@pypi//:requirements.bzl", "requirement")` users working, and the
returned labels route through
{obj}`--@rules_python//python/config_settings:venv`. The `all_*` lists are not
provided, since they could only list one hub's packages; load them from a
concrete hub.
